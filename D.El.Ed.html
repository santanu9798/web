Diploma in Elementary Education (D.EL.Ed.)

Diploma in Elementary Education

The D.El.Ed. (Diploma in Elementary Education) is a professional teacher training program designed specifically for individuals who want to teach young learners in primary and upper primary schools (Classes 1 to 8).


What Do You Study? (Syllabus Overview)

  • Professional teacher education program
  • Experienced and qualified faculty members
  • Smart classrooms and modern teaching aids
  • School internship and teaching practice
  • Educational workshops and seminars
  • Personality development and communication skills
  • Library and learning resources
  • Focus on child-centered education

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand educational philosophies and principles
  • Apply effective teaching-learning methods
  • Manage classrooms efficiently
  • Develop lesson plans and assessment strategies
  • Use educational technology in teaching
  • Foster inclusive and child-friendly learning environments

Why Choose Us?

  • Quality Teacher Education
  • Modern Infrastructure
  • Practical Teaching Experience
  • Student-Centered Learning Environment
  • Experienced Faculty Members
  • Career-Oriented Training

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must have completed Graduation or Post-Graduation from a recognized university.
  • Minimum percentage requirements shall be as per university and government regulations.
  • Admission is subject to the rules and regulations prescribed by the affiliating university and regulatory authorities.

Course Duration & Structure

Duration: 2 Years (Four Semesters)

The program thoroughly integrates:

  • Theory Classes
  • Practical Training
  • School Internship
  • Teaching Practice
  • Project Work
