About Dr. Jaymangal Singh Prathmik Prashikshan Sansthan

Dr. Jaymangal Singh Prathmik Prashikshan Sansthan is a reputed teacher-training institution located in Bhupiamau, Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh. The institute is dedicated to preparing competent, skilled, and socially responsible educators who can contribute effectively to the development of quality education in society.

We believe that teachers play a vital role in nation-building. Therefore, our programs emphasize innovative teaching methodologies, classroom management, child psychology, educational technology, and hands-on teaching practice.

The institute provides a supportive learning environment with qualified faculty members, academic resources, and opportunities for personal and professional growth.

🎯 Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in teacher education by developing competent, innovative, and socially responsible educators who contribute to the advancement of education and society.

🚀 Our Mission

  • Provide quality teacher education.
  • Promote academic excellence.
  • Develop ethical values and leadership.
  • Encourage innovative teaching practices.
  • Prepare future-ready teachers.
