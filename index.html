About Our Institution

Welcome to Dr. Jay Mangal Singh Prathmic Prashikshan Sansthan, located at Vill & Post – Bhupiyamau, Dist – Pratapgarh (U.P.). We are a dedicated teacher training college offering programmes designed to build the next generation of outstanding primary school educators.

This institution stands as a beacon of educational excellence in the Pratapgarh region. Our website serves as the primary source of news, information, and updates about our programmes, facilities, activities, and important archives. We are proud of the wide variety of academic programmes designed to help people succeed in life through meaningful education.

Our College offers a wide variety of academic programmes designed to help people succeed in life and live a life of significance through education.
